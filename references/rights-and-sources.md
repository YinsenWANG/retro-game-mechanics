# Sources, rights and research boundaries

Read this file before acquiring or unpacking any third-party game artifact.

## General rule

Public availability, abandonware labels, emulator support, visible source, a downloadable ROM or a game being old do not by themselves grant permission to copy, retain, unpack, modify or redistribute it.

This Skill is not legal advice. Record the actual permission basis and keep analysis within it.

## Acquisition priority

1. Use a local artifact supplied by the user with a stated right to inspect it.
2. Use creator/publisher-released source, demos, SDK samples or explicitly licensed game packages.
3. Use lawfully dumped personal media when the user confirms the applicable permission and local rules.
4. Use public manuals, creator commentary and recordings when the game binary or source cannot be lawfully obtained.

Do not bypass access controls, DRM, authentication, paywalls or service restrictions. Do not automate a service when its terms prohibit automated access.

## Rights manifest

Record before extraction:

- game, creator/publisher, platform, release, region and revision;
- source URL or local provenance;
- license or exact permission statement;
- why inspection and local retention are allowed;
- SHA-256 or stronger hashes for original artifacts;
- whether modification, redistribution, commercial use, training or public display is allowed;
- verification date and unresolved questions.

Use three states:

- **Green**: source or artifact is explicitly licensed for the intended inspection and retention.
- **Yellow**: local inspection is allowed but redistribution or modification is not; publish behavioral analysis, not copied code, data, maps, graphics or audio.
- **Red**: provenance is unclear, access controls would be bypassed, or terms prohibit the intended access; stop acquisition and request an authorized artifact or use public evidence only.

## PICO-8 service boundary

Lexaloffle's Terms of Use, updated 2026-06-22, state that BBS works remain their authors' property by default, redistribution or remix requires permission or an applicable license, and automated access generally requires permission from Lexaloffle and involved authors except for search indexing or archival.

- Terms: https://www.lexaloffle.com/info.php?page=tos
- FAQ: https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php?page=faq

Prefer user-provided local `.p8`, `.p8.png` or `.p8.rom`, author-published repositories, or manual acquisition through PICO-8/SPLORE. Do not bulk-download the BBS or treat its visible “Code” panel as a redistribution license.

## Evidence labels

- **[事实]** authoritative documentation, creator statements, license text or reproducible metadata.
- **[源码/二进制观察]** behavior directly supported by authorized source, disassembly, data, constants, memory or control flow.
- **[游玩观察]** behavior observed in a specified release, platform, emulator, hardware run or recording.
- **[设计推断·高/中/低]** an inferred design role or player effect with supporting evidence.
- **[未知]** information current evidence cannot establish.

Community comments are player-feedback evidence, not creator intent.

## Publication boundary

- Explain behavioral rules, values, formats, state relationships and function/symbol roles when they are necessary to understand mechanics.
- Do not reproduce complete copyrighted source, ROM regions, maps, sprites, music, text or other expressive assets merely because they were extractable.
- Do not bundle third-party game artifacts or extracted assets in this Skill or its repository.
- Preserve attribution and license links for every sample discussed.
